the project

About GD Levels Archive

What this site is, why it exists, and how it's maintained.

What this is

GD Levels Archive is a fan-made, hand-curated collection of Geometry Dash level files (.gdr, .gdr2, .gmd). Every entry ships with its Level ID, creator, verifier, song, rating, and more — a proper reference, not just a download link.

Why it exists

Level files disappear — accounts get deleted, links rot, showcases get taken down. This archive exists so a well-documented copy of these levels, with full context, keeps existing somewhere.

How it's maintained

There's no public upload form or account system. Every level is added directly to the site's source by the maintainer after being reviewed, which keeps the archive spam-free and consistent.

Want a level added?

Send a submission with the level name, ID, creator, and file type — every request is reviewed by hand.
